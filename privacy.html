Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Sakura Hermes Automation ("this application") is a personal automation tool operated by a single individual for their own private use. It is not offered as a public service and does not collect, store, or process data on behalf of any third party.

What data is accessed

This application connects to the operator's own Google account via OAuth to perform automated tasks the operator has configured, which may include:

  • Uploading encrypted/compressed backup archives to a private Google Drive folder owned by the operator.
  • Reading or managing the operator's own Calendar events.
  • Reading or sending email from the operator's own Gmail account.

How data is used

All data accessed is used solely to carry out the automation the operator has configured for themselves. No data is sold, shared, or transmitted to any third party. No data is used for advertising.

Data retention

Data (such as backup archives) remains in the operator's own Google Drive, under the operator's sole control, until the operator deletes it.

Contact

This is a single-operator personal tool. For questions, contact the account owner directly.
